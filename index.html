FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you handle?
+

We handle all types of civil construction projects including residential and commercial buildings (up to high-rise), bridges, culverts, road works, drainage and water supply systems, and infrastructure projects. We also provide structural design, AutoCAD drawings, BOQ preparation, and project management consulting.

How long does it take to receive a quote?
+

After you submit your inquiry, we contact you within 24 hours for an initial discussion. A full written proposal with BOQ and cost estimate is typically ready within 3–5 working days after the site visit, depending on project complexity.

Do you handle government / publicly funded projects?
+

Yes. We have extensive experience with government and internationally funded projects, including KFAED-funded bridge reconstruction for the Road Development Authority and AIIB-funded landslide mitigation projects across six districts. We are familiar with all relevant government procurement and reporting requirements.

What quality assurance do you provide?
+

We implement comprehensive QA/QC procedures for all projects covering materials testing, workmanship inspections, and standards compliance. For road projects we follow SLS and RDA standards. For buildings we follow local authority approvals and structural design codes. Weekly QA reports are provided to clients throughout construction.

Can you work outside Mawanella / Kegalle district?
+

Yes. We have worked across many districts including Kandy, Matale, Kurunegala, Ratnapura, Nuwara Eliya, and others. We are available for projects across Sri Lanka and have previously worked on overseas assignments (Qatar). Travel and mobilisation costs will be discussed transparently in our proposal.

What software do you use for design and planning?
+

We use AutoCAD for design drawings, Staad Pro 2004 and SAP 2000 for structural analysis, Primavera P3 & P6 for project scheduling and resource planning, and MS Project for programme management. This means you get professional, industry-standard outputs for all your documentation needs.

Is the initial consultation really free?
+

Yes — completely free and with no obligation. We believe in understanding your project before any commitment. The initial consultation (phone or site visit) helps us give you an accurate proposal rather than a generic estimate. Only once you approve our proposal and formally engage us do any fees apply.
